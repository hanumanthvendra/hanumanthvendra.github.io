MCP · Kubernetes · FinOps

Bounded power: an AI that right-sizes your cluster — and a platform that won't let it break prod.

The AI decides what to change. The platform decides what it's allowed to do — server-side, where the model can't route around it — and refuses the rest, even when asked directly.

request flow allowed refused audit

Every apply — allowed, refused, or dry-run — is checked against the policy first and appended to the audit log. Reads never touch it.

Three rules the model can't argue with

Pure, unit-tested policy in finops/policy.py. Not a prompt, not a suggestion — the server returns the refusal and there is no phrasing that talks it out of one.

02 · floor

Resource floors

A right-sizing may approach the floor but never cross it — no scaling a service into the ground.

cpu    ≥ 50m
memory ≥ 64Mi
replicas ≥ 1
refuses below floor

Everyone's racing to give AI agents more power. The harder work is giving them bounded power — real actions inside a fence the model can't climb. The intelligence is Claude's. The judgment about what's safe to run stays the platform's.
